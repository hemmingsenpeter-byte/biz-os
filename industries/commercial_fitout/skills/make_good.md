# Make-good playbook

Make good is the tenant's obligation to reinstate premises on lease exit. Scope it from the lease, not from memory.

## Steps
1. Read the make-good clause and any schedule of condition taken at lease start.
2. Separate what must be removed (partitions, cabling, signage, joinery) from what may stay (base-build items the landlord accepts).
3. Scope by area and trade using the price book category `make_good`; list each reinstatement item.
4. Allow for landlord inspection, access times and any approval lead time.
5. Flag any clause that is ambiguous for the owner to read; do not interpret it yourself.

## Clause patterns to look for
- "Reinstate to base-build condition" versus "to the condition at commencement".
- "Remove all tenant cabling" (often includes risers) and "make good ceiling and floor penetrations".
- Cash settlement in lieu of reinstatement, and the notice period for electing it.
- Holding-over and access-for-works periods after lease end.
- Landlord's right to do the works and recover cost.
