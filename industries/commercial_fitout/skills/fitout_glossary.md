# Fit-out glossary

- **Base build**: the landlord's standard shell and services before tenant works.
- **Cat A / Cat B**: Cat A is a base-build finish with ceilings, lighting and services; Cat B is the tenant fit-out on top.
- **NLA**: net lettable area, in square metres.
- **Strip-out**: removal of existing fit-out, ceilings and services.
- **FF&E**: furniture, fixtures and equipment.
- **Make good**: reinstating premises to lease condition on exit.
- **Provisional sum**: an allowance for work that cannot yet be priced; never presented as a fixed price.
- **Variation**: a priced change to the agreed scope.
- **Practical completion**: works complete and usable apart from minor defects.

Proposal language: quote by area and trade, state exclusions and assumptions plainly, show
landlord approvals and out-of-hours access as assumptions, and mark anything unpriced as such.
